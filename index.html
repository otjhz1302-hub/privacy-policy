Privacy Policy

Nova Cleaner · Effective date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Otjh LLC ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Nova Cleaner Android application ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

Nova Cleaner itself does not collect or transmit personal information to our servers. However, the third-party SDKs integrated into the App may collect certain data as described below.

  • Package information: The App requests the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission to display a list of installed apps so you can manage storage. This information is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted externally.
  • Usage and diagnostic data: Crash reports and performance data may be collected by Google Firebase Crashlytics to help us improve app stability.
  • Advertising data: We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to deliver personalized ads in accordance with Google's privacy policies.

2. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy — applies to AdMob advertising and Firebase services
  • Google AdMob — ad serving and measurement

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain the core cleaning and optimization features
  • To display relevant advertisements that support the free version of the App
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app performance

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties except as required to operate the App (e.g., advertising and analytics partners listed above) or as required by law.

5. Children's Privacy

Nova Cleaner is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

6. Your Choices

  • Ad personalization: You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings under Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Permissions: You can revoke App permissions at any time in your device's Settings → Apps → Nova Cleaner → Permissions.

7. Data Retention

We retain crash and diagnostic data only as long as necessary to resolve issues. Advertising data is retained by Google per their own retention policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
